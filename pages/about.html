About Dr. Sneha Keluskar

Dr. Sneha Keluskar is a qualified Homoeopathic physician with a Doctor of Homoeopathy (DHHMC, Pune, 2011) and a Master’s in Homoeopathy from YMT Homoeopathic Medical College, Navi Mumbai (2016). She has also completed specialized certification in Female Disorders and Paediatrics, with sound knowledge of mental health conditions and their holistic management. Her areas of special interest include hair fall, skin problems, paediatric care, geriatric health, PCOD, menopausal concerns, thyroid disorders, anxiety, depression, and various other health conditions. She believes in providing individualized homoeopathic care with a focus on understanding the patient’s overall physical and emotional well-being.
